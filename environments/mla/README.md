# MLA environment

`mla` derives its authoritative `plane1` work frame from the measured UR
Installation Plane Feature `new_board_mla`. The `plane1` origin is the
verified sensor-board center, 53 mm along the Feature's local `+Z`; its
orientation is unchanged.

## Coordinate and sensor contract

- Dashboard measurement:
  `p[-0.08533,-0.48683,0.24284,1.16388,2.91348,0.00029]`.
- `plane1` composes the raw Feature pose with local translation
  `[0, 0, +0.053] m`. No normal correction, in-plane rotation, or wall-derived
  correction is applied.
- The sensor-board center is therefore `plane1 [0, 0, 0] m`, which resolves to
  approximately `base_link [0.0851177, 0.4869043, 0.1898405] m`.
- The protected sensor column covers the complete `0.40 x 0.40 m` footprint
  from local `z=-0.060 m` (60 mm above the sensor along local `-Z`) through
  the sensor and along local `+Z` to the `base_link z=0` tabletop.
- Because the measured plane is slightly tilted, the conservative tabletop
  bound is `z=+0.19096485659512854 m`; this makes the whole lower face reach
  or overlap the tabletop instead of leaving an unprotected corner gap.

Only the sensor-board collision group follows the new work frame. The four
ground slabs, robot base, walls, and top panel retain their previous physical
`base_link` poses and dimensions. The redundant central ground patch
`base_ground_fixed_base_support` is intentionally absent. The fixed walls are
encoded directly in `base_link`, so they do not move when `plane1` is
recalibrated.

No old trajectories, capture-window tables, or recordings are relabelled into
this environment. They carry a different TF/environment hash. Generate new
MLA trajectory authority and complete compile, mock recording, inspect, and
independent playback under `environment_id=mla`.

`trajectories/mla_grid_*.csv`. It covers `x_A,y_A={-0.05,0,+0.05} m` and
